# Pricing Structure

> **Source:** https://docs.shovels.ai/docs/knowledge-base/getting-started/pricing-structure
> **Fetched:** 2026-04-16

## Product SKUs

Shovels offers three distinct products:

1. **Shovels Online** — web interface for individual users. CSV downloads and 12+ months of history.
2. **Shovels API** — programmatic access. Designed for developer integrations; supports 10,000+ calls per month.
3. **Enterprise Data License (EDL)** — custom enterprise solution delivering full datasets via Snowflake / BigQuery / Databricks with all available fields. Best for bulk multi-county contractor analysis.

## Pricing tiers

- **Online and API intro tier:** starting at **$599/month**.
- Current per-tier details require visiting `app.shovels.ai` or contacting sales — the public docs site does not enumerate every tier.

## Free trial

- 250 API requests with no time limit and no credit card required.
- Counted as requests (1 call = 1 request) rather than records.
- See `../14-api-basics/04-credit-limits.md`.

## EDL & custom pricing

- EDL pricing is custom — contact sales.
- Delivered monthly to your data warehouse of choice.

## Decision rubric

Choose **API** for individual lookups and integrations; choose **EDL** for bulk, multi-county analysis where you need the complete dataset with all fields.

A natural person who may be associated with a business as an owner, officer, or contact.
Persons are a subtype of legal entity and may hold Roles at one or more businesses.
GraphQL type: Person
ExampleHoward Schultz is a person connected to Starbucks Corporation as a former CEO and board member.

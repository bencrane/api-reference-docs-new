# Enigma Console Billing and Usage Documentation

> https://documentation.enigma.com/console/billing

## Overview

The Enigma Console's billing section enables users to "manage your subscription plan, track credit consumption, and access invoices" by navigating to Settings > Billing.

## Plan Management

This section displays your subscription details and allows modifications:

- View current plan and features
- Upgrade or modify your subscription tier
- Update payment information
- Configure automatic credit replenishment
- Buy additional credits

**Payment Method**: Users can update credit card details through a secure Stripe integration by selecting the Edit option.

**Auto-Recharge Feature**: This optional functionality automatically purchases credits when balances fall below a specified threshold, with configurable amounts and trigger points.

## Usage Tracking

The Usage section reveals credit consumption patterns:

- **Current balance**: Shows remaining and total allocated credits
- **30-day consumption graph**: Visual representation of usage trends
- **Category breakdown**: Credits consumed by tier—Core (basic attributes), Plus (enhanced attributes), Premium (advanced attributes), and Actions (operational tasks)

Additional credit calculation details are available in the Pricing & Credit Utilization resource.

## Invoice Management

The Invoices section maintains billing history:

- Complete invoice records with dates and amounts
- Payment status visibility per transaction
- Downloadable invoice copies for record-keeping
